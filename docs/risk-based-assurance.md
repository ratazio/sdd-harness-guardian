# Risk-based assurance

The Guardian uses A1 local, A2 elevated and A3 critical/local-policy escalation.
A2 applies to high/unknown risk, public contracts, data migration, trust
boundaries and material UI. A3 names accountable local authority; it is never a
Guardian compliance certification. Choose techniques by claim and risk, record
the oracle/evidence and retain independent evaluation. Gherkin, mutation,
screenshots and coverage targets are contextual options, not universal gates.

Only declared-profile structure is eligible for the focused local mirror. It
does not assess semantic adequacy. ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 informs requirements
traceability; ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119-1 informs general testing concepts; ISO/IEC 25010 informs
quality characteristics; ATAM/QAW inform trade-off and quality-attribute
reasoning; arc42/ADR inform architecture decision recording; NIST SSDF and
OWASP ASVS inform secure-development and verification questions. None makes
the Guardian, or a consumer, certified or compliant.
