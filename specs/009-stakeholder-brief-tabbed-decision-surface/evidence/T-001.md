## Outcome and decision

U-001 and U-002 have a proposed, bounded resolution, pending independent evaluation:

1. Use **native anchors as the baseline**: the eight controls are `<a href="#panel-id">` elements and all eight `<section>` panels are present in source order without a `hidden` attribute in the static HTML. With scripting unavailable, links still navigate by fragment and every decision surface is readable in normal document order.
2. Add an **inline progressive enhancement** only after the document loads: `role="tablist"`, `role="tab"`, `role="tabpanel"`, `aria-controls`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-selected` and roving `tabindex`. It selects one panel by setting `hidden` only at runtime, handles click, ArrowLeft/ArrowRight, Home, End, Enter and Space, and retains the same-file fragment through `history.replaceState`.
3. Print uses `@media print` to reveal every hidden panel. The enhancement also reveals panels on `beforeprint` and restores the active selection after printing. No framework, route, remote asset, storage or new data source is involved.
4. Put the source-sufficiency/question protocol in the existing canonical authoring surface `.harness/templates/stakeholder-brief-design.md`; make `.harness/skills/spec-review/SKILL.md` and `.harness/agents/spec-guardian.md` review it rather than creating a new discovery skill or a duplicate question schema. `scripts/new_initiative.py` remains the single scaffold entrypoint because it copies the canonical `stakeholder-brief.html` template.

## Rejected alternatives

| Alternative | Reason rejected |
|---|---|
| Button/JS-only tabs with panels hidden in static HTML | No-script readers could lose material content. |
| Multiple brief files, router or framework | Violates the one-document/offline scope and adds needless runtime. |
| CSS-only `:target` as the complete interaction | Retains anchors but does not provide the required coherent roving keyboard state/focus contract. |
| New standalone discovery skill or HTML-owned question schema | Duplicates the design/review protocol and creates another source of truth. |
