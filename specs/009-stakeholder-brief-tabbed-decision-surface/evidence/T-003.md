## 1. Outcome and bounded change

T-003 establishes one reusable author/reviewer contract for the eight v2
decision views. It does not create a task, validation or HTML sidecar schema,
does not require a quota of detail, and does not make software architecture or
commands mandatory. The proposed resolution for U-003 is that the stable
execution-card fields are the already-defined, non-empty fields of
`.harness/templates/tasks.md`; `stakeholder-brief.html` remains a derived
projection. The equivalent validation fields are the existing AC trace in
`.harness/templates/validation-plan.md`.

## 3. Material clarification contract

An author asks only when an absent fact blocks or materially changes a requested
decision, acceptance criterion, risk control, authority or next safe step. The
canonical record must contain:

1. the exact needed fact;
2. the accountable owner or bounded discovery task;
3. the decision impact; and
4. the resolution path/checkpoint.

For a non-material absence, the author uses concise source-backed N/A only
when the reader needs the omission explained; otherwise the optional child
detail is omitted. This is the proposed U-003 resolution: a rich card projects
what canonical task/validation sources contain, and missing material does not
silently create a parallel field model.

## 6. Acceptance and residual risk

- AC-002/003/004/007/009: proposed guidance gives each view a concrete mission
  while allowing justified sparse/non-software content.
- AC-005/006: proposed field identification comes from existing task and
  validation templates; T-004 alone may project it into rich rendered cards.
- U-003: implementation evidence proposes the stable field set and absence
  behavior; independent approval is still required before marking it resolved.
- Residual risk IR-003/IR-004: an author could still over-project or ask a
  generic question. The evaluator must reject any missing source locator,
  invented technical detail, unexplained N/A, or question without exact fact,
  owner, decision impact and resolution path.
