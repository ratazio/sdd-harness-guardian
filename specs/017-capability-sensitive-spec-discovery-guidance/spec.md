## 1. Problem

Mock reviews found promises attributed to layers that cannot control them: browser/MDM reset, remote deletion, and crash recovery across infrastructure. The remedy is contextual agent guidance, not a universal technical checklist.

## 2. Objective

Planning agents recognize relevant authority/viability signals and record a mechanism, configurable policy, dependency contract or owned blocking unknown.
