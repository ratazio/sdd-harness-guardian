  `brief_review.review_record`; an unrelated log row cannot satisfy the gate.

  `coverage_reviewer` and `prior_change_anchor` alongside the existing lineage,

- A materially stale v1 brief now says to migrate to v2 or record a reviewed
  legacy exception; the validator never migrates it automatically.

Additional precise fixtures cover row heading/target/disposition/reason,
baseline metadata, the v1 legacy route and propagation false positives. The
