Decision register and next safe step

Approve the direction now; choose the two contract details explicitly instead of allowing the implementation to infer them.

D-005.1 — Design lineage

Recommendation: `data-harness-brief-design="v1"` on the canonical shell, checked by the validator and updated only in a versioned template change.

Alternative: a stylesheet checksum. Rejected because harmless customization becomes brittle.

Consequence: stable and inspectable; still requires a rendered review.

D-005.2 — Custom layout exception

Recommendation: record a reviewed exception in the existing `decision-log.md`, with rationale, owner, retained decision surfaces and review date.

Alternative: a new sidecar file. Rejected unless the validator cannot safely read the log convention.

Consequence: no silent bypass and no default new artifact.

Accepted limitation

A machine can verify lineage, source links and freshness. It cannot prove that a diagram explains the right trade-off or that the page works in a meeting. That remains an independent reviewer responsibility.

Next safe step

If approved: mark the spec ready, derive plan/impact/validation artifacts, then implement the design standard, template changes, validator checks and 004 retrofit as one versioned slice.

Requested approval: approve the proposed direction and the two recommendations above, or replace either recommendation with your preferred convention. That decision unlocks planning without guessing public consumer-contract behavior.
