## 1. Implementation summary

Created an isolated editorial-composition contract for executive stakeholder
briefs and a distinct review contract. The contract makes source locator,
allowed synthesis, limit/discovery and artifact projection explicit before any
derived architecture narrative. Three calibration corpora preserve different
honest cases: source-supported product architecture, a non-software operational
flow and a macro-only source whose internal detail must remain a discovery.

**Outcome served:** enable a source-backed executive reading when architecture
is material, operational or insufficiently described, without renderer
inference.  
**Demonstrable increment or uncertainty reduced:** a composer/reviewer can now
exchange an inspectable map and calibration fixtures distinguish supported zoom,
operational N/A and required discovery; a discovery whose owner/path are absent
from the source remains explicitly unowned rather than borrowing an adjacent
actor. Conversely, owner/path are recorded when their exact source supports
them. An invalid fixture preserves the fabricated-internal-detail anti-pattern
for review.  
**Expected artifact/behavior produced:** two additive skills, two isolated
roles, fixture corpus and a narrow integrity test. No page, router, CSS,
renderer, historic brief or canonical source fact was altered.
