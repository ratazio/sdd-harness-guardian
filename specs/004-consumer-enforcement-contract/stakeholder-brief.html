Spec 004 · decision brief · updated 2026-08-18 · risk: medium · size: M

Make Human Visibility enforceable in consumer projects without turning the Guardian into a workflow engine.

The bundle will supply a portable validator and adoption contract. Each consumer or future Factory scaffold must invoke that contract through its own instructions and CI/task-runner.

Requested decision: approve the bounded vendor implementation now; decide the concrete Factory template integration only after its command contract is delivered.

Snapshot

OutcomeNon-trivial work cannot silently advance with a missing, stale or structurally invalid brief.
BenefitStakeholders receive a real gate, while projects retain CI/tooling choice.
SizeM: script, fixtures, state contract and adoption guidance.
Smaller approachTemplate-only lint is insufficient because consumers could still skip it.

Focused slice

In scope

Stdlib validator; Git/base-ref plus local hash-baseline freshness check; reviewed exception; generic invocation guidance.

Deliberately out of scope

Hosted workflow engine, CI-provider adapter, LLM/prose judge, screenshot CI and a real Agentic Factory change.

Architecture

Text equivalent: source artifacts, run state and brief flow into the vendor validator; its categorized result is invoked by a consumer-local wrapper or CI; an independent reviewer still decides semantic/rendered readiness. This boundary prevents the vendor from owning the consumer workflow.

Execution flow

Text equivalent: author updates source artifacts and brief; validator checks structure, gate state and freshness; an independent reviewer checks meaning and rendered legibility; only then may task readiness be granted. A machine pass never replaces the reviewer.
