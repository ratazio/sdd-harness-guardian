Stakeholder decision brief

Make Human Visibility enforceable in consumer projects without turning the Guardian into a workflow engine.

Supply a portable validator and adoption contract while consumers retain local invocation, instructions and CI ownership.

Requested decision

Approve the bounded vendor contract; defer concrete Factory integration until a consumer command contract exists.

Owner/status: platform-engineering · accepted scope, implementation evidence pending.

Snapshot · Scope · Architecture · Impact · Validation · Decision

Outcome

Non-trivial work cannot silently advance with a missing, stale, invalid or bare-page brief.

Benefit

Stakeholders receive a real gate while consumers retain tooling choice.

Size

M: validator, fixtures, state contract and guidance; this is proportionality, not an estimate.

Smaller approach

Template-only lint was rejected because consumers could still skip it.

Review trigger

Review after consumer command and fixtures pass; source status is current.

Focused slice

Portable enforcement, not a hosted workflow engine.

In scope

Deliberately out of scope

Concern/audience/purpose: architects need the vendor/consumer ownership boundary.

Text equivalent: source artifacts, run state and brief enter the vendor validator. A consumer wrapper or CI invokes it. An independent reviewer still evaluates meaning and rendering; the vendor is not the consumer workflow engine.
