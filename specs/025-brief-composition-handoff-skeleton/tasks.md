## T-001 — Make the editorial handoff executable

**Outcome:** the existing composition section becomes a route/block handoff a
composer and reviewer can pass between themselves.  
**Scope:** templates, guidance, workflow and fixtures; no candidate promotion.  
**Exit:** map records source, target, fact/absence, form and state; independent
review finds no second source of truth or lost material heading.

## T-002 — Materialize a non-promotable skeleton

**Outcome:** a reviewed map becomes an initiative-specific HTML skeleton with
every slot visibly `a_preencher`.  
**Scope:** skeleton mechanism, lifecycle labels and fixtures; no factual prose
generation.  
**Exit:** skeleton has eight routes/slots, retains source links, fails promotion
and cannot be represented as a rendered brief.

## T-003 — Protect composition integrity without prose scoring

**Outcome:** stable map targets, task IDs, proof IDs, placeholders and
provenance are checked before promotion.  
**Scope:** narrow validators/fixtures and renderer integration.  
**Exit:** rich fixture and M-003 expose task/proof cards; missing links or
placeholder fail by default. A current editorial omission may proceed only
through a visible, exact-review-bound, expiring exception; integrity,
provenance, lifecycle and safety fail unconditionally; qualitative approval
remains external.

## T-004 — Prove the handoff with a fresh M-003 candidate

**Outcome:** M-003 demonstrates a complete executive brief through the single
canonical path.  
**Scope:** fresh disposable root, M-003 sources/map/skeleton/candidate/render,
captures and independent review; no mutation of prior lab/history.  
**Exit:** reviewer recovers impact, five tasks, seven proofs, decisions and
recovery/rollback; regressions and preservation hashes pass.

## T-005 — Make the visual construction contract real

**Outcome:** the reusable template and every instantiated skeleton already
show the decision narrative and the visual components the composed brief will
use. The composition agent fills a deliberate structure; it never has to
invent the page shape from a row saying only "card" or "topology".

**Scope:** deepen `plan.md` as the sole Markdown construction handoff; replace
the generic visual template and the SPEC 025 skeleton with eight actual
subpages and their empty component models; preserve the non-promotable
lifecycle of the instance. This is domain-generic: the structure supports
software, operations, policy, research and non-software work through
source-backed component selection or explicit absence.

**Exit:** desktop inspection shows one route at a time; each route has an
executive question, narrative arc and visibly shaped slots; architecture has
global view/pillars/domain zooms; execution has epic/task dossiers; validation
has assurance pillars/proof dossiers/acceptance criteria. Structural tests
verify the route and slot contract; an independent reviewer judges whether the
result is understandable and faithful rather than scoring prose or beauty.

## T-006 — Compose from the skeleton, not beside it

**Outcome:** a fresh, non-promoted SPEC 025 candidate is an authored copy of
the approved skeleton, not a parallel HTML implementation.  It uses the
canonical sources to show the architecture of this workflow as a landscape of
the solution and a distinct lifecycle/control flow.

**Scope:** add the generic base-copy and context-selected architecture rule to
the contract; copy the SPEC 025 skeleton into the candidate location; replace
only source-backed slots with authored narrative and visual components;
declare the base path/hash; review and validate the candidate.  No renderer
promotion, state gate opening or mutation of historical briefs.

**Exit:** the candidate identifies its skeleton base, has no material
placeholder, shows one route per `?view=`, and separates a source-backed
solution landscape from the relevant information/control flow where both are
material.  An evaluator distinct from the composer can recover all six tasks,
ten validation checks, impacts, authority and limits without accepting it as a
delivered brief.
