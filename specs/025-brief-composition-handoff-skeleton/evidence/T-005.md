## Outcome and boundary

T-005 replaces the shallow lifecycle fixture with the reusable
`executive-brief-v3` visual construction contract.  It does **not** promote a
new stakeholder brief or create factual content for a particular initiative.
The SPEC 025 instance remains a deliberately non-promotable skeleton whose
visible slots say `a preencher`.

## Implemented contract

- The canonical template and SPEC 025 skeleton each provide eight true routed
  desktop subpages: scope, architecture, impact, execution, validation,
  evolution, decision and coverage.  A `?view=` route makes one panel current;
  it is not an anchor-scroll document.
- Both use `data-harness-brief-structure="executive-brief-v3"`.  The selected
  instance uses the Pearson profile; the bundle template remains
  vendor-neutral.
- Architecture has an executive thesis, pillars, global topology and legend,
  bounded domain zooms, contracts, changed/not-changed surfaces and a visible
  distinction between deterministic controls and human judgement.
- Execution has an epic arc plus a complete task dossier model.  The instance
  materializes `#task-T-001` through `#task-T-005`.
- Validation has assurance pillars, a flow, proof dossiers, acceptance/oracle
  fields and a matrix.  The instance materializes `#proof-V-025-01` through
  `#proof-V-025-09`.
- `plan.md` maps the new material slots (`execution.T-005` and
  `validation.V-025-09`) and defines the generic construction record, component
  dossiers and explicit-absence rule.

## Desktop inspection and independent decision

The builder inspected the architecture, execution, validation and scope views
at desktop width.  They present one route at a time, retain the persistent
shell and show intentionally empty component models rather than a long page of
paragraphs.  The independent evaluator approved the structural and editorial
contract after checking the source, plan map, routes, slots and ownership
boundary; its finding is recorded in `decision-log.md#D-025-011`.

The evaluator did not turn this into a quality score or a substitute for a
stakeholder meeting.  A future composer must still read that initiative's
canonical Markdown, choose the proportionate visual expression and author the
filled HTML/CSS/JS before guarded promotion.

## Agent-authorship boundary

The final composition is agent-authored.  Scripts may copy/instantiate a blank
shell, verify structure/provenance and promote exact reviewed bytes only.  They
must not summarize Markdown, select diagrams or component forms, or generate
the narrative/architecture/task/proof blocks.  This boundary is defined in
`plan.md` and the composer guidance.
